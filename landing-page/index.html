Features

Everything you need to play
Drive videos on your desktop

DrivePlayer bridges Google Drive and your favorite local video player, with zero compromise on privacy.

100% Private

No backend, no servers. All data is stored locally on your machine. Your Google credentials never leave your device.

VLC & IINA Support

Stream directly to your preferred local video player. Native support for VLC and IINA with seamless playback.

Browse & Search

Browse your Google Drive files and folders seamlessly. Search for any video file with instant results.

Easy API Setup

Simple 2-minute Google Cloud Console setup with step-by-step guidance built right into the app.

Cross-Platform

Available for macOS (Apple Silicon & Intel) and Windows. Run DrivePlayer on the platform you prefer.

Open Source

Fully open source on GitHub. Inspect, modify, and contribute to the code. Transparency you can trust.

How It Works

Up and running in minutes

Three simple steps to start streaming your Google Drive videos locally.

1

Configure API

Set up your own Google Cloud credentials in 2 minutes with the built-in guide.

2

Connect Account

Authorize your Google account securely. Credentials stay on your device.

3

Play Videos

Browse your Drive, pick a video, and stream it instantly in VLC or IINA.
